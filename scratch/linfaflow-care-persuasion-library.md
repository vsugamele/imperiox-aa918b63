# LinfaFlow Care Persuasion Library

Internal conversion library for the web intake/chat flow. Lead-facing copy remains in English; internal notes can be reviewed in Portuguese.

## Psychological Avatars

| Avatar key | Lead signal | Core emotional job | Conversion angle |
|---|---|---|---|
| `normal_tests_invisible` | Normal labs, doctor said nothing is wrong | Validate the pattern without claiming diagnosis | "Normal tests do not always explain how your body feels by the end of the day." |
| `external_fix_prisoner` | Compression, elevation, drainage, creams | Reframe failed effort as wrong layer, not personal failure | Outside-in relief vs inside-support routine |
| `standing_all_day` | Teacher, nurse, salon, retail, long shifts | Respect that she cannot stop/rest | 30-second ritual that fits real life |
| `body_confidence_withdrawal` | Photos, shoes, clothes, hiding, mirror | Name gradual retreat from life/body | Practical 30-day step toward comfort/confidence |
| `silent_bloating` | Belly, face, morning puffiness | Connect puffiness + heaviness as one daily pattern | Systemic drainage-support explanation |
| `mysterious_swelling` | Repeated pattern, unclear cause | Make the experience legible | Timing, triggers, daily pattern, then mechanism |
| `safety_review` | Sudden, one-sided, painful, medication/condition | Pause sales | Medical review before supplement decision |

## Deep Recognition Lines

- "You are not asking for hype. You are trying to understand why the same pattern keeps coming back after normal daily life."
- "The frustrating part is not that compression or elevation never help. It is that the same pattern can come back when real life starts again."
- "This often shows up slowly: different shoes, different clothes, fewer photos, and calling it a phase because explaining it feels exhausting."
- "You cannot build a routine that depends on lying down when your day does not let you stop."
- "Normal tests can make you feel like you have to prove what your body is already showing you by the end of the day."
- "You are not asking to be sold to. You are asking to be heard about something no one has named yet."
- "The hardest part is not the swelling. It is the slow retreat from photos, plans and shoes you used to love - and the way that retreat never gets announced."
- "If your body is showing you something the labs did not, it deserves an answer that is not a guess in a white coat."

## Safe Proof Assets

- Outside-in vs inside-support explanation.
- Cleavers-led ingredient hierarchy: Cleavers, Stillingia, Prickly Ash, Red Clover.
- 1 mL twice daily, 30-day routine explanation.
- Situational triggers: standing, sitting, travel, heat, salty meals, sleep disruption.
- Non-diuretic positioning: not a water pill, not cleanse-aggressive, no urination claims.
- Risk boundary: not diagnosis/treatment; consult doctor/pharmacist for medications, pregnancy, diagnosed conditions, sudden/severe swelling, chest symptoms, wounds, severe pain.
